# Engineerium - Complete Project Summary

## 🚀 Project Overview

**Engineerium** is an interactive STEM education platform that teaches engineering concepts through 3D visualizations, gamified lessons, and AI-powered tutoring.

**Target Audience:** Students, hobbyists, and anyone interested in learning about rockets, planes, cars, electronics, mathematics, and physics.

---

## ✅ What We've Built

### 1. **Core Learning Modules**

#### Rockets 🚀
- 20+ lessons covering rocket fundamentals to orbital mechanics
- Topics: Thrust, ISP, staging, orbital transfers, gravity assists
- Interactive 3D rocket models with detailed components
- Quizzes and progress tracking

#### Cars 🚗
- 20+ lessons on automotive engineering
- Topics: Vehicle dynamics, powertrains, aerodynamics, systems design
- 3D car models with interactive components
- Real-world examples and calculations

#### Planes ✈️
- Aviation engineering lessons
- Topics: Aerodynamics, lift, drag, propulsion
- 3D aircraft models
- Flight mechanics and calculations

#### Electronics ⚡
- 20+ lessons on electronics and robotics
- Topics: Circuits, motors, embedded systems, automation
- Practical projects and applications

#### Mathematics 📐
- Engineering mathematics curriculum
- Topics: Calculus, differential equations, linear algebra
- Applied to real engineering problems

#### Physics 🔬
- Physics for engineers
- Topics: Mechanics, thermodynamics, electromagnetism
- Practical applications

### 2. **Interactive Features**

#### 3D Model Viewer
- High-quality 3D models of rockets, planes, and cars
- Interactive rotation, zoom, and pan
- Component highlighting and annotations
- Procedurally generated Falcon 9 rocket model
- GLB model support for planes and cars

#### Calculators
- Rocket Equation Calculator (Tsiolkovsky)
- Orbital Transfer Calculator (Hohmann transfers)
- Lift Calculator (aircraft)
- Real-time calculations with visual feedback

#### Gamification System
- Progress tracking across all modules
- Achievement system with badges
- XP and leveling system
