## ⚠️ RISKS & CHALLENGES

### **High Risk Factors**
1. **Competition** - Khan Academy, Coursera launch similar features
2. **User Acquisition Cost** - Marketing more expensive than expected
3. **Conversion Rate** - Users don't convert to premium (below 2%)
4. **Churn** - Premium users cancel after 1-2 months
5. **Technical Issues** - Platform downtime, bugs
6. **Content Quality** - Need constant updates and improvements

### **Mitigation Strategies**
1. **Unique Features** - 3D annotation system is hard to copy
2. **Organic Growth** - Focus on SEO, content marketing, community
3. **Value Proposition** - Make premium tier irresistible
4. **Retention** - Gamification, progress tracking, achievements
5. **Reliability** - Invest in infrastructure, monitoring
6. **Content Pipeline** - Hire part-time educators/creators
